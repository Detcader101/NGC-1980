THE ISLAND WAR

THE ISLAND WAR — your carrier ALBATROSS against the enemy carrier KRAKEN. Islands give fuel (F), materiel (M) or radar sight (R) to whoever holds them. You only see what your helicopter, ships and islands can see.

TAKE ISLANDS: knock out their AA guns, then land on the pad and hold it — or send a capture drone. Cave fortresses fall when you steal the pod from their cave. Sink the KRAKEN or take its headquarters IRONHOLD to win; lose the ALBATROSS and the war is lost.

The enemy's factories are building a battleship — bomb the factories and its shipyard to hold it back.

M command map: click the sea to sail the carrier there, click an island to select it · 1 capture drone · 2 AA gun on our island · 3 new helicopter · 4 Tomcat escort (top cover). Bombs land on the yellow ring. Land on the carrier or any friendly pad to refuel, repair and rearm.

DOWNED? Set down on land and press L: a dropship brings a para repair team. Fly the jump run (A/D steer, W/S height, F jump — the green smoke shows the wind), then steer the canopies (A/D, S brakes). Hands off, they do it themselves. Stuck for good? Esc → respawn on the carrier.
