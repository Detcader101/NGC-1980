CHANGE 02

Local combat voices and interruption

Nearby Marines speak from live positions. Distance and wall/door intersections change gain and low-pass filtering. Urgent calls stop lower-priority sources; pause, OFF and out-of-range events cancel real audio.

What the evidence shows

The contact image shows Marines near the landed Huey and a contact/corpsman subtitle. Separate audio checks move a listener, insert a wall, interrupt a line and stop the voice bus.

  • Live distance fading, wall low-pass, out-of-range cancellation and radio-only mode passed.
  • Pause/OFF, zero volume, queue age/bounds and no-system-voices silence passed.

Limits of this check

  • The scene positions units and delivers deterministic hostile-shot and field-care events through game hooks. It does not validate autonomous combat tactics.
  • Wall acoustics approximate obstruction, without diffraction, room reverberation or carrier roll/pitch transforms.
CHANGE 03

Speak an addressed order

Hold F9, say the called station, THIS IS COMMAND, one supported order, OVER, then release. The live roster lists callers and capabilities; typed calls use the same parser. Units reply WILCO/OUT or UNABLE with their existing voice identity.

What the evidence shows

A controlled final microphone transcript waited for release, then changed a real Marine squad to hold. A typed call then ordered that squad to defend Sandbar. Invalid procedure and paused-game calls did not mutate orders.

  • 8/8 real-game command assertions.
  • 38/38 parser, recognition lifecycle and dispatch checks, including ambiguity, cancellation, permission/network errors, stale units and SAY AGAIN without repeating the action.

Limits of this check

  • Recognition was driven by a test provider. A physical microphone, accents/noise and remote browser speech services need manual testing.
  • This is a bounded military command-net convention, not a validator of every legal aviation transmission. Only listed unit orders are supported.
  • Browsers may send microphone audio to their own speech service; the panel discloses this before use.
